01 — The gap

Doctor visits are monthly.
Health problems are daily.

2 visits / mo

What the care system actually sees. Between them: everything is on the patient to mention.

60 sec / day

What Cura asks for. A phone call's worth of facts — no app skills, no typing, no remembering.

Days too late

When families usually find out. "You should have told me sooner" is the saddest sentence in eldercare.

Cura fills the gap between visits — with a daily voice check-in that decides, by grade, who gets called.
