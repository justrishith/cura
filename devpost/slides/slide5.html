04 — Safety, by construction

The model only proposes. The server enforces.

CRISIS

Never touches the agent. A deterministic phrase screen routes chest-pain-style phrases to a scripted path: calm the user, notify family — and the agent gets no say in it.

HIGH

Family is notified, every time. Caregiver alert runs even if the model never proposes it. A model trying to fire crisis_alert on a non-crisis turn is downgraded — the emergency script cannot leak.

LOW

Gentle, useful. A plain-language health tip plus a graded summary the family can read — nothing urgent, nobody called.

Every turn is auditable — tool receipts, check-ins, alerts and call outcomes persist in Supabase, replayable on the caregiver dashboard.

Cura never calls 911.

Not a disclaimer — an architecture rule. Escalation to emergency services is a human press, always. Demo is mocked data and clearly labeled.
