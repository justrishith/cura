02 — How it works

One call's worth of facts, three jobs.

1

Mom talks for 60 sec

Daily voice check-in — real speech, out loud replies. No account, no setup, nothing to remember. Speech-to-text plus an elder-sized console.

graded by the agent 2

Cura grades the day

A Gemini agent turn reads the words, sets a grade — low · high · crisis — and assembles tools. The server, not the model, decides what is allowed to fire.

3

Phones ring. Family watches.

High grade → Cura offers to call the doctor and does — a real Twilio call. The caregiver dashboard streams check-ins, alerts and booked appointments live.

Every turn is auditable: receipts stamp what happened, and the family can answer or acknowledge from anywhere.
